Guardian Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Guardian is a browser extension that blocks adult and scam web pages for children.

What Guardian does with your data

Nothing leaves your device. Guardian does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information, browsing history, page content, screenshots or URLs. It has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising.

How it works

When you open a web page, Guardian reads the page text and takes an image of the visible tab. A classification model and text recognition (OCR) run inside the browser, on your device, to decide whether the page is adult or scam content. The image and text are used only for that check and are discarded afterwards.

What is stored on your device

Guardian saves the following in the browser's local extension storage, on your device only:

  • Your settings (for example, which protections are turned on).
  • A salted hash of the parent PIN. The PIN itself is never stored.
  • Your allowed-site and blocked-site lists.
  • A history of pages Guardian has blocked (site name, time and confidence). Ordinary browsing is never logged.

You can clear this history from the Activity tab, and removing the extension deletes all of it.

Permissions

Guardian asks for access to all websites so it can check any page a child might open. This access is used only for the on-device check described above.

Third parties

Guardian does not send data to any third party and does not load code from remote servers.

Children

Guardian is a tool for parents to protect children. It does not collect any information from children or from anyone else.
